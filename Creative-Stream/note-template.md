# Note template

Copy this structure exactly. Replace `<…>`. Keep every heading, even when a section is empty.
Tasks use Obsidian Tasks syntax: `+ [ ] <task> <priority> 📅 YYYY-MM-DD`, priority one of 🔺 ⏫ 🔼 🔽 ⏬.

````markdown
---
status: capturing | probing | crossing | weighing | reported | specced
sessions: <n>
created: <YYYY-MM-DD>
updated: <YYYY-MM-DD>
tags: [ideas, creative-stream]
---
# <Topic>

## Session state
- Phase: <phase>
- In focus: <I-id and what is happening, e.g. "I2, Q3/5 asked">
- Open questions: <list>
- Next session starts with: <the exact next step>

## Ideas
| ID | Idea | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | <one line> | pursue / park / kill / — |

## I1 — <title>
- Origin: problem-first | solution-first
- Angles: <lens>: <what came out> (one line per lens)
- Facts: <claim> [user] / [memory] / [source: <url>] / [assumption] / [open]
- Weigh: YAGNI <…> · Ready-made <…> · Takes <…> · Cost <time · tokens · money> · Against <…>
- Verdict: <pursue | park (revisit when …) | kill> — <reason>
- Pitch: <Domain> struggles with <problem> because <reason>. <Approach> by <mechanism>, which works because <insight>.
- SMART: <goal with absolute date>   (pursue only)

## Cross
- <I-a + I-b>: <combination, conflict, or overlap>

## Tasks
+ [ ] <next step> 🔼 📅 <YYYY-MM-DD>

## Parked / killed
- I<n> — <verdict>: <reason>; revisit when <trigger>

## Session log
- <YYYY-MM-DD> — <what happened in one line>
````
